Selling to someone you know?

Most sellers who call me already have a buyer: a neighbor, a friend, a relative or a tenant. With no listing and no bidding, the hard part is agreeing on a price that feels fair to both of you.

An independent appraisal settles that. You both get the same number, backed by recent sales, from someone who doesn't work for either side. The conversation stays about facts, not feelings.

An appraisal helps you:

Agree on a fair price. One independent number, backed by real sales.

Keep it friendly. Nobody is left wondering if they got a fair deal.

Plan for the buyer's loan. If they're getting a mortgage, you'll have a good idea what to expect from the lender's appraisal.

Getting ready to list?

Price too high and your home sits. Price too low and you leave money on the table. Online estimates can't see inside your home, and the data behind them isn't always right. I've seen online listings off by hundreds of square feet.

An appraisal gives you an independent value before you set your price, whether you're selling on your own or with an agent.

How it works

  1. Free consult. We'll talk about your home, your buyer if you already have one, and your timeline. I'll give you an exact quote upfront.
  2. On-site inspection. The visit usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. I document the home's features, condition, and any updates that add value.
  3. Report delivery. Your report is usually done within 3 to 5 business days, with a clear, well-supported value.

Common questions

My buyer is getting a mortgage. Do I still need this?

If you want to agree on a price first, yes. The buyer's lender orders its own appraisal for the loan, but that happens after you've agreed on a price. Mine gives you both a fair number to start from.

What if the value is lower than I expected?

Better to know now than after you've agreed on a price or listed. You'll have time to adjust, take care of condition issues, or see which updates buyers will actually pay for.

How long is an appraisal good for?

An appraisal reflects the market when it's done. In a fast-moving market, try to have it done within 90 days of your sale or listing.

How much does it cost?

$550 for most single-family homes, townhomes and condos. Unique homes, acreage or multi-family may cost more. I'll quote it upfront.
