Meet the Person
Behind the Data

Hi, I'm Ben Goheen. With over 25 years in the Minnesota market, I've learned that every property has a story. My goal is to understand the unique characteristics of your home, from that custom kitchen renovation to the way your neighborhood school impacts value.

I also believe that an appraiser should be accessible. That means when you call, you speak to a human. When you have questions about your report, I take the time to answer them. I combine the technical rigor of a large firm with the customer service of a small local business.
