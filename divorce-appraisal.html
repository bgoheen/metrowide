Why the appraisal matters

The home is usually the biggest asset on the table. A shaky number, or no appraisal at all, can cost you thousands and keep the arguments going. An independent appraisal gives both of you one defensible number, so the focus can stay on a fair settlement.

A court-ready appraisal helps you:

Avoid disputes. An independent value takes opinion out of the process.

Divide property fairly. Both sides work from the same credible number, and it holds up in mediation or court.

How it works

  1. Free consult. Call or schedule online. I'll answer your questions and give you an exact quote. No pressure, no obligation.
  2. On-site inspection. I'll find a time that works for you and anyone else living in the home. The visit usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. I measure the home, document its condition, and note any updates that affect value.
  3. Report delivery. Your court-ready report is usually done within 3 to 5 business days. It's written in plain language that both of you and your attorneys can follow.
  4. Support if needed. If your attorney or mediator has follow-up questions, I'm available. I stand behind my work.

What's in the report

A full analysis of the home's market value based on recent comparable sales, an interior and exterior inspection, and a clear written explanation of how I arrived at the value. Every report meets USPAP (Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice), the professional standard courts and attorneys rely on.

Common questions

Do both attorneys need to agree on the appraiser?

Not necessarily. One person can order a single appraisal that both sides use, or each side can order their own. A shared appraisal usually costs less and avoids two conflicting values.

What if the other side disputes the value?

My reports are detailed and fully documented, so they hold up in mediation or court. If needed, I can testify or answer written questions from attorneys.

How much does a divorce appraisal cost?

$550 for most single-family homes, townhomes and condos. Unique homes, acreage or multi-family may cost more, and I'll quote it upfront.

How soon can you schedule?

Usually within a few business days, and the report typically follows 3 to 5 business days after the inspection. Call 651-505-4400 or schedule online for an exact timeline.
