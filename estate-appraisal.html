Why the appraisal matters

The home is often the most valuable, and most emotional, asset in an estate. A shaky number, or no appraisal at all, can lead to disagreements among heirs, tax headaches and delays. An independent appraisal gives everyone one defensible number to work from.

A professional estate appraisal helps you:

Handle probate, trust and tax needs. You get a certified report built for the purpose, from a licensed appraiser.

Keep the peace. An independent value takes personal opinion out of the conversation.

Date-of-death vs. current value

Most estates need one of two values. A date-of-death value shows what the home was worth on the day your loved one passed. A current value shows what it's worth today, which is useful for dividing the property among heirs or buying out a sibling. I'll help you figure out which one you need before you order.

A date-of-death value matters most when the family sells the house: it sets the stepped-up cost basis, which can lower the capital gains tax owed. It's also used for estate tax filings.

It doesn't matter how long it's been. I can value the home as of the date of death, whether that was last month or a few years ago.

Common questions

Do I need a current or a date-of-death appraisal?

It depends on the purpose. If you're dividing the home among heirs at today's value, you need a current appraisal. If you're setting the cost basis for a future sale or filing an estate tax return, you need a date-of-death appraisal. I'll help you sort it out during the free consult.

What if the house has changed since the date of death?

That's common, since many homes get cleaned out or fixed up. I appraise the home as it was on the date of death, using photos and details from the family.

What if the house is vacant?

Not a problem. I work with executors and attorneys all the time and can coordinate access to vacant homes. Just let me know the situation when you call.

How much does an estate appraisal cost?

$550 for most single-family homes, townhomes and condos. Unique homes, acreage or multi-family may cost more, and I'll quote it upfront.

How soon can I get the report?

Usually within 3 to 5 business days of the inspection. If there's a filing deadline, let me know and I'll do my best to meet it. Call 651-505-4400 to talk timing.
