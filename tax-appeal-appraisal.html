Why you need more than a hunch

Assessors value thousands of homes at once, and they can miss what's specific to yours: condition, layout, location on the block, or features that limit its appeal. Online estimates and gut feelings won't move an appeal board. A certified appraisal backed by recent sales will.

A tax appeal appraisal helps you:

Present credible evidence. A certified appraisal carries far more weight than an online estimate or your own opinion.

Know before you file. You'll see whether the market actually supports a lower value.

Minnesota appeal deadlines

Deadlines depend on how you appeal. Local and county boards of appeal meet in the spring, and the deadline to file with the Minnesota Tax Court is April 30 of the year the taxes are due. Check your valuation notice for exact dates.

Don't wait: you want the report in hand before you file. I cover Hennepin, Ramsey, Dakota, Anoka, and Washington counties.

How it works

  1. Free consult. We'll look at your assessed value and whether the numbers suggest a real case. I'll give you an honest read before you commit to anything.
  2. On-site inspection. The visit usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. I document the home's condition and features.

Common questions

Is it worth appealing?

It depends on how far off the assessment is. If your home is assessed at $450,000 but is worth closer to $400,000, a successful appeal could lower that year's taxes by several hundred dollars. The appraisal is $550, so the bigger the gap, the better the math.

What if I lose the appeal?

You're out the cost of the appraisal, and you'll still know what your home is actually worth.

How soon should I act?

As soon as you get your valuation notice. Call 651-505-4400 so there's time to finish the appraisal before your deadline.
